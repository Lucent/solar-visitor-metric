Are interstellar visitors alien probes?

Three objects from other stars have crossed the solar system since 2017. A mission would leave traces that chance rarely does: flybys close enough to the planets to borrow their gravity, and a route from one star to another with the Sun as a stop between. Both are scored against random arrivals at the same speed, in bits: each bit halves the odds, so 10 bits is 1 in 1,000. Together scores the two as one test, which costs a little for having looked in two places. None of the three left either trace.
