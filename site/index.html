Are interstellar visitors alien probes?

Three objects from other stars have crossed the solar system since 2017. A probe would give itself away by passing close to several planets on one trip, or by flying a route between stars as a self-replicating probe would. Like a traveling salesman, it would favor the nearest next stop. Each trace is scored in bits of surprise against a null hypothesis of random arrivals at the same speed. Each bit halves the odds: 10 bits is 1 in 1,000. 1I and 2I left neither trace. 3I passed three planets more closely than chance usually allows, but came from no star and is heading for none.
